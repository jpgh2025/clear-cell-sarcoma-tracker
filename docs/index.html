About the disease疾病概况

Two rare tumours, one shared genetic driver.两种罕见肿瘤，同一个基因驱动。

Clear cell sarcoma (CCS)透明细胞肉瘤（CCS）

A rare soft-tissue sarcoma that most often starts near the tendons and aponeuroses of the arms and legs, usually in adolescents and young adults. It shares some features with melanoma under the microscope (it often expresses S100, SOX10, HMB-45 and Melan-A), which is why it was once called "melanoma of soft parts", but it is a different disease with a different cause.

一种罕见的软组织肉瘤，最常发生在四肢的肌腱和腱膜附近，多见于青少年和年轻成人。它在显微镜下与黑色素瘤有相似之处（常表达 S100、SOX10、HMB-45、Melan-A），因此曾被称为"软组织黑色素瘤"，但它是一种病因完全不同的独立疾病。

Gastrointestinal neuroectodermal tumour (GNET)胃肠道神经外胚层肿瘤（GNET）

Formerly called "clear cell sarcoma-like tumour of the gastrointestinal tract" (CCSLGT). It grows in the wall of the stomach or intestine and carries the same EWSR1 fusions as CCS. It is S100 and SOX10 positive but, unlike CCS, usually lacks melanocytic markers such as HMB-45 and Melan-A.

过去称为"胃肠道透明细胞肉瘤样肿瘤"（CCSLGT）。它发生在胃或肠壁内，携带与透明细胞肉瘤相同的 EWSR1 融合基因。免疫组化 S100、SOX10 阳性，但与 CCS 不同，通常不表达 HMB-45、Melan-A 等黑色素细胞标志物。

Standard sarcoma chemotherapy has shown limited benefit in advanced disease晚期患者对常规肉瘤化疗获益有限

Because both tumours share the same driver, research on one is often relevant to the other, and some trials accept "EWSR1-rearranged" tumours. Every trial sets its own eligibility rules, so always check with the study team.由于两者驱动基因相同，一种疾病的研究常对另一种也有参考价值，部分试验接受"EWSR1 重排"肿瘤入组。但每个试验的入组条件不同，请务必与研究团队确认。

Phase II trial · 2026II 期试验 · 2026NCT03277924

IMMUNOSARC II: sunitinib + nivolumabIMMUNOSARC II：舒尼替尼 + 纳武利尤单抗

The largest prospective trial run specifically in clear cell sarcoma. It combines an anti-angiogenic drug (sunitinib) with a PD-1 immunotherapy (nivolumab).

迄今专门针对透明细胞肉瘤开展的最大规模前瞻性临床试验，联合抗血管生成药物（舒尼替尼）与 PD-1 免疫治疗（纳武利尤单抗）。

Retrospective study · 2022回顾性研究 · 2022

World Sarcoma Network: how standard treatments performed世界肉瘤网络：常规治疗的实际效果

55 patients with molecularly confirmed CCS from 10 centres. Standard soft-tissue sarcoma treatments gave limited benefit. The best response rate was seen with sunitinib (3 of 10 patients).

来自 10 个中心、经分子检测确诊的 55 例透明细胞肉瘤患者。常规软组织肉瘤治疗获益有限，舒尼替尼的客观缓解率最高（10 例中 3 例）。

The authors concluded that access to clinical trials remains key for this disease.

Phase II trial · did not meet goalII 期试验 · 未达主要终点

EORTC 90101 "CREATE": crizotinib (MET inhibitor)EORTC 90101 "CREATE"：克唑替尼（MET 抑制剂）

Because the EWSR1 fusion can switch on the MET gene, this trial tested the MET inhibitor crizotinib. Only 1 partial response was seen among 26 evaluable patients. A follow-up lab study found MET was rarely truly activated in these tumours, which may explain why.

由于 EWSR1 融合可能激活 MET 基因，该试验评估了 MET 抑制剂克唑替尼。26 例可评估患者中仅 1 例部分缓解。后续研究发现这类肿瘤中 MET 真正被激活的情况很少，这可能解释了疗效有限的原因。

Source来源: Cancers 2021 · PMID 34885165

Phase II trialII 期试验

Atezolizumab (anti-PD-L1) in chondrosarcoma and CCS阿替利珠单抗（抗 PD-L1）治疗软骨肉瘤和透明细胞肉瘤

A US phase II immunotherapy study. Eligibility includes a documented EWSR1::ATF1 or EWSR1::CREB1 fusion or confirmed clear cell sarcoma. Check the study page for its current status.

美国的一项 II 期免疫治疗研究，入组条件包括检出 EWSR1::ATF1 或 EWSR1::CREB1 融合，或确诊透明细胞肉瘤。当前招募状态请查看研究页面。

Source来源: Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, study 21-022

Laboratory research实验室研究

Targeting the fusion itself直接针对融合基因

Early lab studies are looking for ways to switch off EWSR1::ATF1 directly. In cell and animal models, the HDAC inhibitor vorinostat lowered EWSR1::ATF1 levels, with BRD4 involved. Another study (a preprint, not yet peer reviewed) flagged PRMT5 as a possible weak point. These have not been shown to work in patients.

早期实验室研究正在寻找直接"关闭" EWSR1::ATF1 的方法。在细胞和动物模型中，HDAC 抑制剂伏立诺他可降低 EWSR1::ATF1 水平，机制与 BRD4 有关。另一项研究（预印本，尚未经同行评审）提示 PRMT5 可能是潜在弱点。这些方法尚未在患者中证实有效。

Sources来源: Cancer Res Commun 2023 · PMC10317042 · bioRxiv 2022 (preprint) · PMC10147623

Questions to ask the care team可以向医疗团队提的问题

  1. Has the EWSR1 fusion, and its partner gene, been confirmed?
  2. Has the case been discussed at a sarcoma multidisciplinary team (MDT) meeting?
  3. Which clinical trials could fit, including in other cities or countries?
  4. If the disease is limited to a few spots, is local treatment (surgery, ablation or stereotactic radiotherapy / SBRT) an option?
  5. How will we know if treatment is working, and when is the next scan?
  1. EWSR1 融合基因及其伙伴基因是否已经确认？
  2. 病例是否经过肉瘤多学科会诊（MDT）讨论？
  3. 有哪些临床试验可能适合，包括其他城市或国家的试验？
  4. 如果病灶数量有限，是否可以考虑局部治疗（手术、消融或立体定向放疗 SBRT）？
  5. 如何判断治疗是否有效？下一次复查是什么时候？

Finding trials yourself自己查找临床试验

ClinicalTrials.gov is the largest international registry. In China, use the official drug trial registry. Sarcoma centres also run studies that may not appear online yet, so it is worth asking them directly.

ClinicalTrials.gov 是全球最大的试验登记平台。国内请使用官方的药物临床试验登记与信息公示平台。各肉瘤诊疗中心也可能有尚未在网上公布的研究，值得直接咨询。

Checking what you read如何核实看到的信息

Trustworthy claims can be traced to a PubMed ID (PMID) or trial number (NCT…). Be cautious with treatments sold online that promise results, and bring anything you find to the treating team before acting on it.

可靠的信息能追溯到 PubMed 编号（PMID）或试验编号（NCT…）。对网上宣称有效的疗法要保持警惕，任何发现都请先与主治团队讨论再做决定。

About this site关于本站

How it works运作方式

An automatic script runs every morning (about 08:00 Hong Kong time). It queries the official PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov databases and copies titles, dates and statuses exactly as published, each with its PMID or NCT number and a link. No AI writes or summarises the live sections, so nothing there can be invented. If a database cannot be reached, the site says so and keeps the last good data.

每天早上（香港时间约 8:00）自动运行一次程序，检索官方的 PubMed 和 ClinicalTrials.gov 数据库，原样复制标题、日期和状态，每条都附有 PMID 或 NCT 编号及链接。试验和文献部分不经过任何 AI 撰写或总结，因此不会凭空捏造内容。如果某个数据库无法访问，页面会显示提示，并保留上一次的有效数据。

Not covered automatically未自动覆盖的内容

China's drug trial registry (it has no public data feed) and conference abstracts (ASCO in June, ESMO in September–October, CTOS in November) are not tracked automatically. Check them by hand.

国家药监局的临床试验登记平台（没有公开数据接口）以及学术会议摘要（6 月 ASCO、9–10 月 ESMO、11 月 CTOS）暂不自动追踪，请手动查阅。
